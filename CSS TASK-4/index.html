CSS Box Model Demo

Every box is made of: content → padding → border → margin.

Content Padding Border Margin (empty gap)

Part 1 – Width, Padding and Border

Default box-sizing: content-box: the width property sets only the content width. Padding and border are added on top.

W1 width:200 | padding:0 | border:0 → total 200px
W2 width:200 | padding:20 | border:0 → total 240px
W3 width:200 | padding:20 | border:5 → total 250px
W4 width:200 | padding:50 | border:15 → total 330px
W5 width:200 | padding:10px 60px | border:2px 20px → total 200+120+40 = 360px

Same padding (20px) and border (5px), different content widths:

W6 content 100 → total 150px
W7 content 300 → total 350px

With box-sizing: border-box the width includes padding and border, so the total stays 200px and the content shrinks.

W8 border-box 200 | padding:20 | border:5 → content 150px
W9 border-box 200 | padding:50 | border:15 → content 70px

Part 2 – Height, Padding and Border

Same idea applied to height. Boxes are side by side so the height difference is easy to compare.

H1 h:100 p:0 b:0
total 100
H2 h:100 p:20 b:0
total 140
H3 h:100 p:20 b:5
total 150
H4 h:100 p:40 b:15
total 210

Different content heights, same padding (20px) and border (5px):

H5 h:50
total 100
H3 h:100
total 150
H6 h:180
total 230

box-sizing: border-box with height:

H7 bb h:100 p:20 b:5
content 50
H8 bb h:100 p:40 b:15
content 0 (text overflows/hidden)

Part 3 – Margin (outside the border)

Margin adds space around the box but is not part of its painted area.

M1 margin 0
M2 margin 30px 0 30px 60px
