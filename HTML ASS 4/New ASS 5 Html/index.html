Task 4: Make list of Semantic and Non-semantic tags and show these on your web page.

Semantic and Non-Semantic HTML Tags

Semantic Tags

Semantic tags clearly describe the meaning and purpose of the content.

  • <header> - Header of a page or section
  • <nav> - Navigation links
  • <main> - Main content of the page
  • <section> - A section of content
  • <article> - Independent article or content
  • <aside> - Additional or side content
  • <footer> - Footer of a page or section
  • <figure> - Self-contained media/content
  • <figcaption> - Caption for a figure
  • <time> - Represents a date or time
  • <address> - Contact information
  • <details> - Additional details
  • <summary> - Heading for details

Non-Semantic Tags

Non-semantic tags do not clearly describe the meaning or purpose of their content.

  • <div> - Generic block container
  • <span> - Generic inline container
